# Weekly Report

Read system/COMPANION.md first.

1. Distinguish capture, candidate review and report drafting. A normal conversation item is not automatically a report candidate; ask before capturing it unless the owner explicitly selected it.
2. Save only the approved summary, week, source/provenance, category and stated outcome or follow-up under personal/vault/reports/candidates using templates/report-candidate.md. Do not copy raw work-system content without the owner's retention permission.
3. For a draft, confirm the reporting week and use approved candidates plus bounded owner-supplied context. Do not retrieve additional mail/Teams automatically.
4. Use templates/weekly-report.md: Outcomes, Decisions, Risks and dependencies, Next week and Support needed. Omit empty sections and separate suggested actions from agreed commitments.
5. Return an explicitly labelled draft and save a versioned copy under personal/outputs/weekly-reports unless session-only was requested. State included/excluded candidates and missing inputs.
6. Never send, publish or update an external report. Scheduling remains off until the owner configures one approved scheduler and its scope through companion-care.
