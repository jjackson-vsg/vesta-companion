# companion contacts

1. Search existing contact files under personal/vault/contacts before creating a new one. Disambiguate people with similar names using user-supplied professional details.
2. Capture only supported facts: name, role, organisation, preferred contact method, interaction date, relevant context and source. Ask before saving sensitive personal observations; do not infer personality, protected characteristics or relationship quality from silence.
3. Use templates/contact.md. Keep drafted communications marked draft. A prepared reply is not evidence it was sent.
4. Link related project and decision notes. Store actionable commitments in personal/todo/tasks.json with a source reference, owner, due date when confirmed and stable id.
5. Use cited chronology to answer what changed or what remains open. State gaps honestly. Do not contact anyone or look through unrelated mail without scope.
6. Save to local personal files, verify read-back and add one journal entry. Never upload a contact book to the public repository.

Success: an accurate, source-linked contact record, no duplicates and no fabricated interaction.
